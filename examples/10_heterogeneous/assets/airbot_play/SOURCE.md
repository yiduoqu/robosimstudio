# AIRBOT Play model provenance

The MJCF and OBJ meshes in this directory come from [DISCOVERSE](https://github.com/discoverse-dev/DISCOVERSE), commit `d67f47c084aba0e0cf422a8725235f8b9238655a`, path `models/mjcf/manipulator/new_airbot_play/`. The accompanying `LICENSE` is the upstream MIT license.

The local `airbot_play.xml` drives each parallel finger with a direct position actuator instead of driving one finger through a general actuator and an equality constraint, and removes that equality constraint. Newton's MJCF importer exposes direct position actuators as joint targets, which RSS needs for scene control. It also reshapes the two finger-pad colliders, sets collision friction to 2, and adds one thin shelf to each fingertip for the toy's loop. Matching visual boxes expose this contact adapter in rendered scenes. The native-pad run made bilateral loop contact but lost it as soon as the arm rose; the adapted pads also require further work to carry it. The six arm joints, original visual meshes, inertias, and arm actuators remain from the source model. Meshes were placed under `assets/`, matching the upstream XML's `meshdir`.
